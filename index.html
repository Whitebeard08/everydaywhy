Everyday Why

Everyday Why makes short animated explainers about the everyday questions you've always wondered about, like why the sky is blue or how a fridge makes things cold, and publishes them to our own YouTube channel.

Everyday Why Publisher is our internal tool. It uploads our own videos to our own channel and reads that channel's statistics. It isn't offered to other users.
