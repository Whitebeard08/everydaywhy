Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Everyday Why Publisher ("the app") is an internal tool. Its only use is publishing videos to the YouTube channel owned by Everyday Why, and reading that channel's own statistics.

What the app accesses

Our own YouTube channel, through the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, to upload videos and read view and engagement statistics. No data about any other person. The app doesn't sign in other users, and doesn't collect, sell or share personal information.

Storage

Access tokens and the statistics we read are stored privately on our own server and used only to run our channel. They are never shared with third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
